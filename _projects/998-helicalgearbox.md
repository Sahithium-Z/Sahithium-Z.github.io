I designed a helical gearbox using Solidworks in order to practice working with gears and gear ratios. The project contained over twenty parts including the cover, gears, cuffs, and keys. The gearbox includes two stages with a total gear ratio of 10:1. Since I am now more experienced with gears, my next step is to design a transmission.
